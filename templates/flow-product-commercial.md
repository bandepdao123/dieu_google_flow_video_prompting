# Flow product commercial

## Product locks
Master asset, geometry, proportions, material, exact color, orientation, label zone and state.

## Prompt
```text
[Commercial shot/lens] of the exact referenced product [position/action] in [locked set]. Keep its geometry, proportions, material and color unchanged. [Camera move]. [Controlled product/environment motion]. [Lighting, reflections, palette and texture]. SFX: [...]. End on [hero pose/frame].
```

Avoid uncontrolled hand contact. Flag text/logo fidelity for inspection or post-production. Do not let style reference alter product geometry.
