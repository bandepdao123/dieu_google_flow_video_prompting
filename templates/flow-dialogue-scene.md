# Flow dialogue scene

## Locks
Character identity/wardrobe, voice profile, eyeline, spatial positions, room layout, light direction.

## Prompt
```text
[Shot composition] of [speakers in locked positions]. [Speaker ID] [action] and says, "[short dialogue]" in [delivery]. [Listener reaction]. The camera [one move or locked-off]. Preserve eyelines and screen geography. SFX: [...]. Ambient noise: [...]. End with [pose/gaze/emotion handoff].
```

Keep dialogue within duration. For multiple speakers, split shots/clips when lip-sync, eyeline or continuity risk becomes high.
